Consent, privacy and the full product

This concept demo does not visit the links you enter, connect to your accounts or send them for analysis. The fields remain in this page, without a saved history. Every finding comes from Jane’s prewritten fictional case study.

The proposed full product adds ID verification, a live face match and account sign-in before consent. On-device processing and immediate deletion of identity data are design goals. These features are not implemented in this demo.
